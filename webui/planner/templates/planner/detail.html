Economics

Capital cost annualized at {{ run.params.discount_rate_pct }}% over each technology's own lifetime, versus the risk-adjusted operating cost. The capital recovery factor is the share of a technology's capital cost paid each year, so that equal yearly payments repay it with interest over its lifetime: r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1) at discount rate r and lifetime n years. The annualized capital cost is the capital cost times this factor.

Green hydrogen

Hydrogen bought on the market is not green. Each site covers its minimum green share of the hydrogen it keeps (made plus bought minus sold) with renewable electricity certificates (Guarantees of Origin). One certificate is 1 MWh of renewable electricity and covers the hydrogen an electrolyser makes from it, {{ electrolyser_efficiency }} MWh. Every MWh of the site's own wind and solar output earns a certificate. All values are certificates, in MWh of electricity per year{% if run.params.scenarios|length > 1 %}, averaged over the scenarios{% endif %}.
