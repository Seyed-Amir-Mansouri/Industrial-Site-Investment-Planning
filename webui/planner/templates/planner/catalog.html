Maximum capacity per site

Each asset's default maximum size at a site follows that site's demand, so that the asset can cover on its own every peak it can serve: a factor times the site's annual peaks of the demands the asset serves, rounded up to a whole number of its smallest product and then to a whole MW. Heat and cooling assets and their stores get 25% headroom over the peak they serve, so they can cover it with some margin. The biogas CHP and the industrial heat pump also count the heat that drives the absorption chiller (both cooling peaks divided by its 0.7 COP), and the CHP's heat is converted to MW of electricity (0.40 / 0.45). With these defaults every demand an active asset firmly serves is covered and the plan leaves nothing unmet. Wind, PV and the battery are allowed several times the electricity peak because they also sell to the market. The electrolyser is sized in MW of electricity, so its hydrogen peak is divided by its 68% efficiency. A demand with a 0 MW peak gives its assets a 0 MW default. On the New run page each site's Max MW box starts from this rule and follows the peaks until you enter your own value, and an asset that isn't active at a site gets 0 MW there. The rule is CAP_RULES in site_investor_planning/config.py.

Green hydrogen and certificates

Hydrogen bought on the market is not green. Each site sets its own minimum green share of the hydrogen it keeps over the year (bought plus made by its electrolyser minus sold) and covers it with Guarantees of Origin: bought, or kept from its own wind and solar output. One Guarantee of Origin covers the hydrogen an electrolyser makes from 1 MWh of electricity ({{ site.electrolyser_efficiency }} MWh). Every MWh of the site's own wind and solar output earns a Guarantee of Origin, whether the site uses that electricity or exports it; the site sells the ones it doesn't keep.
