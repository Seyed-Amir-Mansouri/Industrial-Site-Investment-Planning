What the coverage colors mean

The default Max MW of each asset follows the site's peaks; the rule is explained on the Catalog page.

Covered the active assets can supply every peak at once at the most their Max MW lets the catalog products build, counting the CHP's heat and the absorption chiller once across all the demands they serve, so the plan leaves none of it unmet; Partly firm capacity is below the peak, or only solar or recovered heat serves it; Unmet nothing active serves it, so all of it is charged as unmet demand; Market bought from the grid or the hydrogen market; No demand.

05Uncertainty scenarios

These scenarios model electricity price uncertainty arising from changes in the European electricity generation mix. Specifically, they represent deviations in installed wind and solar photovoltaic (PV) capacity across European countries relative to the 2030 ENTSO-E Ten-Year Network Development Plan (TYNDP) National Trends baseline. These changes influence wholesale electricity price formation and, consequently, industrial investment decisions.

For each country, enter the percentage change in installed capacity relative to the baseline: +20% means 20% more capacity than planned, while −20% means 20% less. Keep each value within the trained range shown below its input field. Scenario probabilities must sum to 100%.
