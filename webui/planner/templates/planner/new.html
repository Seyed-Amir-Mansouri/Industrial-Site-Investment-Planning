04Uncertainty scenarios

The Baseline is planned on its own by default (100%, deterministic). Add as many scenarios of your own as you like to plan under uncertainty: each has a probability and a wind and solar error per country compared with the Baseline (the share of the site's own wind/solar output lost), and uses Baseline market prices. Probabilities must add up to 100%; with two or more scenarios the risk measure switches to CVaR. Changes apply to this run only.
